Dear Kriti, 💌

There are people who are kind when it's easy. And then there's you — kind when it's inconvenient, kind when it costs you something, kind to people who will never learn your name. I've watched you do it my entire life and it still astonishes me.

You cannot see a soul in pain and walk away. Not a crying child, not a tired stranger, not a stray at the edge of the road. Something in you simply refuses. I used to think that was softness. I've grown up enough to know it's the bravest thing about you.

You give like someone who has never once counted — your time, your food, your last bit of patience, your whole heart handed over before anyone has to ask twice.

So here's the one thing I need you to hear today, and then I'll stop being sentimental: the person who carries everyone else deserves to be carried too. This year, let us. Let me.

Happy birthday to the gentlest, most generous, most stubbornly good human in this family. The world is luckier than it realises — and I'm the luckiest of all, because I get to call you my sister. 🌻

with all my love, always — your Babu ❤️
